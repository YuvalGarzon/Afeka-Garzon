Rome, Italy

Rome, the capital of Italy, is a captivating city steeped in history and brimming with vibrant culture. From the ruins of the Roman Empire to the grandeur of Vatican City, Rome offers an unparalleled journey through time. Its charming streets, delicious cuisine, and iconic landmarks make it a must-visit destination for travelers worldwide.
