Tel Aviv, Israel

Tel Aviv, often dubbed the "City That Never Sleeps," is a vibrant and modern metropolis on Israel's Mediterranean coast. Founded in 1909 as the Jewish cultural center outside Jaffa, it's known for its beautiful beaches, Bauhaus architecture, and lively nightlife. Visitors are drawn to its unique blend of history, culture, and innovation.
