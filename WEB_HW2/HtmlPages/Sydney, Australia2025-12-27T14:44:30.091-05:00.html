Sydney, Australia

Sydney, the capital of New South Wales, is a vibrant and cosmopolitan city known for its iconic harbour and stunning beaches. Founded in 1788 as a British penal colony, it has evolved into a global hub for culture, finance, and tourism. With its unique blend of history, modern architecture, and natural beauty, Sydney offers an unforgettable experience for every visitor.
