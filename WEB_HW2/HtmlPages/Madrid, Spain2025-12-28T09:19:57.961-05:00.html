Madrid, Spain

Known for its stunning architecture, world-class museums, and lively nightlife, it's a city that effortlessly charms visitors. From the grandeur of the Royal Palace to the bustling atmosphere of Plaza Mayor, Madrid promises an unforgettable experience.
